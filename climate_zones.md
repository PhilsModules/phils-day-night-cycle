## Appendix: Moon Phases

The moon cycle (default 30 days) influences the night brightness. Below are the default phases and their effects:

| Phase                  | Days       | Solar Offset | Illumination | Description                       |
| :--------------------- | :--------- | :----------- | :----------- | :-------------------------------- |
| 🌑 **New Moon**        | 1-2, 29-30 | 0h           | 0.00         | Invisible. Darkest nights.        |
| 🌒 **Waxing Crescent** | 3-6        | +9h          | 0.05         | Visible in the late evening.      |
| 🌓 **First Quarter**   | 7-10       | +10h         | 0.10         | Sets around 04:00 AM.             |
| 🌔 **Waxing Gibbous**  | 11-14      | +11h         | 0.20         | Bright part of the night.         |
| 🌕 **Full Moon**       | 15-16      | +12h         | 0.30         | Brightest nights (Midnight Peak). |
| 🌖 **Waning Gibbous**  | 17-20      | +13h         | 0.20         | Bright. Rises in the evening.     |
| 🌗 **Last Quarter**    | 21-24      | +14h         | 0.10         | Rises around 20:00 PM.            |
| 🌘 **Waning Crescent** | 25-28      | +15h         | 0.05         | Visible until early morning.      |


## Appendix: Brightness Levels

The module calculates scene darkness based on the sun's position. Here are the brightness levels for the key times:

| Time Point | Brightness | Darkness | Description                                                   |
| :--------- | :--------- | :------- | :------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Dawn**   | 0%         | 1.0      | Start of sunrise. Light **gradually increases** towards Noon. |
| **Noon**   | 100%       | 0.0      | Maximum brightness. Sun is at its peak.                       |
| **Dusk**   | 50%        | 0.5      | Sunset. Light **gradually decreases** towards Night.          |
| **Night**  | ~5%        | 0.95     | Full night darkness (modified by moon phase).                 |

> **Note:** The light transitions **smoothly** between these points (no sudden jumps).
